# Kite 社区行为规范 / Community Code of Conduct

[中文](#中文) | [English](#english)

## 中文

Kite 希望建立一个专业、友善、重视事实和安全边界的技术社区。本规范适用于仓库、Issues、Pull Requests、Discussions，以及代表 Kite 参与的其他公开协作空间。

### 我们鼓励

- 围绕事实、复现、消费场景和可验证结果讨论；
- 对想法、代码和论证提出具体意见，不攻击个人；
- 主动说明不确定性、利益关系、第三方来源和风险；
- 尊重新人、非开发者和不同经验背景，耐心解释必要上下文；
- 发现错误后及时修正，避免把争论升级为身份冲突；
- 保护凭据、隐私、安全漏洞和未公开的敏感信息。

### 不可接受

- 骚扰、威胁、歧视、羞辱、持续人身攻击或恶意围堵；
- 冒充他人、伪造证据、故意隐瞒来源、依赖、外联或安全影响；
- 发布他人隐私、凭据或未协调披露的可利用漏洞；
- 垃圾信息、无关推广、欺骗性商业招揽或反复扰乱讨论；
- 利用社区身份要求所有权、收益、特殊待遇或绕过审查；
- 在被要求停止后继续破坏性互动。

### 处理方式

维护者可以提醒、编辑或隐藏不当内容、要求停止、锁定讨论，或在严重和持续情况下限制参与。处理应尽量依据可见行为、影响和重复程度，并避免公开不必要的私人信息。

遇到明显违规内容，可以使用 GitHub 的内容举报功能。若安全且不涉及隐私，也可以在相关对话中提醒 `@Yokin-XY`；涉及漏洞请使用 [安全政策](SECURITY.md)中的私密渠道。不要为了举报而再次公开敏感信息。

## English

Kite aims to maintain a professional, welcoming technical community grounded in evidence and security boundaries. This policy applies to the repository, Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and other public spaces where someone represents Kite.

### Encouraged behavior

- Discuss facts, reproduction, use cases, and verifiable outcomes.
- Critique ideas, code, and reasoning specifically without attacking people.
- Disclose uncertainty, conflicts of interest, third-party sources, and risk.
- Respect newcomers, non-developers, and different experience levels; explain necessary context patiently.
- Correct mistakes promptly and avoid turning technical disagreement into identity conflict.
- Protect credentials, privacy, vulnerabilities, and non-public sensitive information.

### Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, humiliation, sustained personal attacks, or targeted disruption.
- Impersonation, fabricated evidence, or deliberate concealment of provenance, dependencies, network behavior, or security impact.
- Publishing another person's private data, credentials, or exploitable vulnerability details without coordination.
- Spam, unrelated promotion, deceptive commercial solicitation, or repeated disruption.
- Using a community title to demand ownership, revenue, special treatment, or review bypass.
- Continuing harmful interaction after being asked to stop.

### Enforcement

Maintainers may warn participants, edit or hide inappropriate content, request that behavior stop, lock conversations, or restrict participation in serious or repeated cases. Enforcement should be based on visible behavior, impact, and repetition while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of private information.

Use GitHub's content-reporting tools for clear violations. When safe and free of private information, mention `@Yokin-XY` in the relevant conversation; use the private channel in the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md#english) for vulnerabilities. Do not republish sensitive information while reporting it.
